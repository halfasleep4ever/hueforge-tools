TD Meter

Print a small step card in your filament. Photograph it against a bright light. Get an estimate of its TD.

1. Print the step card

  1. Slice with exactly these layer heights. Print in one filament.
  2. 100 % infill, no ironing, no "adaptive layers".
  3. Print it flat on the plate, smooth side down.

2. Measure

  1. Hold the card flat against a bright window, or a phone screen showing plain white. Fill the frame. No direct sun.
  2. Take the photo straight on, with no flash.
  3. Drop it here. Then click the open window, then steps 1 to 8. Or drag one line from the window centre to the step 8 centre.
Drop the photo here, or click to choose

Type the brightness of each part, in any unit (for example a light meter, or the value from a photo editor in linear light). Leave a box empty to skip that step.

This is an estimate. The tool fits T(t) = e−kt to your steps and reports the thickness where only 3 % of the light gets through. Light lost at the surface is fitted separately and left out of the TD. HueForge's own TD definition may differ slightly, so compare against a known filament once.
