What is TD?

TD (Transmission Distance) tells HueForge how see-through a filament is. It is the thickness in mm at which the filament looks almost fully opaque. A low TD (black, about 0.5) blocks light fast. A high TD (white or yellow, 4 to 6) lets light through and mixes with the colours under it. Measure yours with the TD Meter.

Import takes: this site's JSON export, CSV with columns name,brand,hex,td, or a HueForge filament library JSON.
